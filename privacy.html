Privacy Policy - Babaclipz Uploader

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Babaclipz Uploader ("the app") is a private, single-user desktop tool that the channel owner runs on their own computer to upload short gaming clips to their own YouTube channel and to read that channel's video statistics. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • With the youtube.upload permission: it uploads videos, titles, descriptions and tags to the authorized channel.
  • With the youtube.readonly and YouTube Analytics read-only permissions: it reads views, likes, comments and watch-time figures for the videos it uploaded.

The app does not access other users' data, private messages, subscriber lists or payment information.

How data is used and stored

  • Statistics are used only to show the channel owner which clips perform best and to choose future clips.
  • The OAuth token and the statistics are stored only on the owner's computer, in local files. Nothing is sent to servers controlled by the developer; there are none.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Statistics obtained from the YouTube API are refreshed or deleted within 30 days.

How to revoke access and delete data

  • Revoke the app's access at any time from Google security settings.
  • Deleting the app's folder on the computer deletes the token and every stored statistic. After access is revoked, stored API data is deleted within 7 days.
